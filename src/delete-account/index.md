# Delete Your GoalPlate Account

**GoalPlate** (developer: Eric Vicente Zepeda Juarez) lets you permanently delete your account and its associated data at any time. You can do this directly in the app — no email or support request needed.

_Last updated: July 26, 2026_

> [Leer esta página en español](/delete-account/es)

## How to delete your account in the app

1. Open **GoalPlate** and sign in.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab.
3. Tap **Settings**.
4. Tap **Delete my account**.
5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Deletion takes effect immediately. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke GoalPlate's access with Apple as part of the process.

## Can't access the app?

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, email us at **support@goalplate.app** from the email address associated with your account and request deletion. We will process the request and confirm within 30 days.

## Delete some of your data without deleting your account

You can also permanently delete individual pieces of your GoalPlate data in the app, while keeping your account:

- **Saved recipes** — My Recipes → the ⋮ menu on a recipe → Remove (recipes you shared to the community can be unshared or removed everywhere)
- **Meal plans** — Meal Plan → the ⋮ menu on a plan → Delete
- **Goals and their check-ins** — Goals → the ⋮ menu on a goal → Delete (removes the goal and all check-ins logged on it)

These deletions are immediate and permanent. For anything not covered above, email **support@goalplate.app** and we will handle the request within 30 days.

## What is deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently delete all data associated with it, including:

- Your account and profile (name, email address, sign-in identifiers)
- Fitness profile details you provided (age, height, weight, activity level)
- Your goals, check-ins, streaks, and achievements
- Your saved recipes and meal plans, including generated images
- Recipes you shared to the community feed (removed from Discover)
- Notification/device tokens and app preferences
- Usage counters (monthly quotas)

## What is kept, and for how long

- **Purchase and subscription records** are maintained by Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) in their own systems under their policies — GoalPlate never stores your payment details. Deleting your account does **not** cancel an active subscription: manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
- **Diagnostic logs** (error reports) that referenced your account are retained for up to **90 days** in our monitoring system, then deleted automatically.
- We may retain minimal records where required by law (for example, records of a prior deletion request).

For more detail, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
